The Clinical Imperative

Why HridyaVayu? The Case, The Need & The Impact

Transforming conventional reactive asthma management into proactive, AI-guided attack prevention.

The Clinical Problem (The Need)

Over 300 million individuals globally suffer from chronic bronchial asthma. Over 80% of severe hospitalizations are triggered by undetected ambient air pollution spikes (PM2.5, NO2, SO2) interacting with hyper-reactive airways. Conventional asthma care is purely reactive—patients take medication only after airway constriction has already begun.

The HridyaVayu Breakthrough (The Case)

HridyaVayu bridges continuous satellite and ground-station IoT environmental telemetry with validated clinical guidelines (GINA criteria). By applying collaborative multimodal machine learning (combining Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting), the system predicts elevated exacerbation risk hours before symptom onset, giving patients a critical preventive window.

The Patient & Physician Impact (The Benefits)

Patients receive personalized, actionable care directives, real-time smart inhaler canister tracking, and immediate emergency SOS dispatch during acute flare-ups. Clinicians gain objective, longitudinal population telemetry, verifiable medication adherence records, and calibrated risk scores backed by deterministic medical safety rails.

Rigorous Clinical Generalizability

Benchmarked Across 3,402 Real Patients Worldwide

The HridyaVayu collaborative architecture has been independently validated on the Zenodo Clinical Cohort (Haque et al., 1,010 patients: 92.57% Accuracy, 0.942 F1) and the Kaggle Multi-Center Demographics Dataset (Elkharoua et al., 2,392 patients: 96.45% Accuracy, 0.958 F1).

The Indian Epidemiological Emergency

India's Escalating Asthma Crisis & Urban Smog Reality

According to The Lancet Global Health, India accounts for 42.4% of all global asthma deaths despite having only 11.1% of global cases. In Indian megacities, winter ambient PM2.5 levels routinely cross 250 to 450+ μg/m³—up to 30 times the WHO safe threshold. HridyaVayu was designed specifically to give Indian citizens proactive early warning before toxic particulate spikes trigger acute attacks.
